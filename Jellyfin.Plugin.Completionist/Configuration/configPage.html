Completionist

Lists the TV seasons and episodes that have aired, and the films from your collections that have been released, but aren't in your library. New ones are posted to Discord.

What's missing

Shows that couldn't be checked
These shows couldn't be matched to an episode guide. Fixing their metadata (Identify, or Refresh metadata) usually sorts it out.
Ignored

Discord notifications

In Discord: channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL. Leave blank to turn notifications off.
Discord only hears about new gaps: an episode that aired or a film that came out since the last check, or something that disappeared from your library. The first check sends a one-message summary instead of everything that's already missing.
A short "now in your library" note when you fill a gap.
An "all clear" after the daily check and Check now, with how much is still missing. Checks after a library scan stay quiet.
If Jellyfin is reachable from the internet, notifications link to the show or collection and show its poster.

TV shows

Gives downloads time to arrive before an episode is reported. 0 reports it as soon as it has aired.
Turn off if you only keep some seasons of a show on purpose. Gaps inside seasons you have are still reported.
Needs a TMDb key: TVmaze doesn't number specials, so they can't be matched.
For shows no episode guide knows, or whose numbering doesn't match (e.g. anime in absolute order): having episodes 1, 2 and 4 means 3 is missing.

Film collections

Every franchise you own at least one film of (from TMDb, the same data Jellyfin's collections use), and every Jellyfin collection with a TMDb id.
TMDb's date is usually the cinema release. Films typically reach streaming and disc about 90 days later.
Free at themoviedb.org → Settings → API. Needed for film collections. For TV it's optional: with a key, TMDb is asked first, which matches the episode numbering Jellyfin's built-in metadata uses and covers specials. Without one, TVmaze is used.

Libraries

Pick the libraries to check. If none are ticked, every TV and movie library is checked.

When to check

Episode lists are cached for 12 hours (7 days for ended shows) and collections for 3 days, so this is quick. Full checks also run from the "Find missing episodes and films" scheduled task (daily at 5am by default, under Scheduled Tasks → Library).

Log

What the plugin has done since Jellyfin last started. The full history is in Jellyfin's server log (Dashboard → Logs).
Start over forgets the report and cached lists. The next check looks everything up again and sends a fresh summary to Discord.
